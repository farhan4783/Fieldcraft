# FIELDCRAFT — 60-Second Demo Video Script

**Theme:** Hacktoberfest 2026 — Week 1 “Touch Grass”  
**Target Video Duration:** 60–90 seconds

---

### Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:08)
- **Visual:** Person looking down at a bright phone screen while walking down a street, tripping slightly or missing trees.
- **Narration (Voiceover):**  
  > “Most outdoor apps still ask you to stare at your screen. FIELDCRAFT gives you a reason to put it away.”

---

### Scene 2: Setup (0:08 – 0:20)
- **Visual:** Screen capture of FIELDCRAFT setup page on mobile or desktop. User selects “20 Min” and “Mystery & Noir”, then taps “Create My Mystery”.
- **Narration:**  
  > “Select your walk duration and an investigative tone. FIELDCRAFT queries OpenStreetMap for public parks, plazas, and landmarks near you.”

---

### Scene 3: AI Proof & Local Privacy (0:20 – 0:32)
- **Visual:** Brief shot showing terminal / Ollama badge: “🟢 Local Gemma 3 Active”. The Case Briefing card loads with title: *“The Whispering Cartographer”*.
- **Narration:**  
  > “Gemma 3 runs locally on your device—spinning real public landmarks into a tailored fictional mystery without sending story prompts to closed commercial AI providers.”

---

### Scene 4: Field Mode & Audio (0:32 – 0:45)
- **Visual:** User taps “Begin Walk”. Audio narration starts. User puts phone in jacket pocket with headphones on. Outdoor footage of walking through a leafy park.
- **Narration:**  
  > “Clues are read aloud through Web Speech. Your phone stays in your pocket as you search for the real-world destination.”

---

### Scene 5: Physical Geofence Gate (0:45 – 0:60)
- **Visual:** Phone screen pulls up near the landmark. The dual-reading geofence verifies arrival within the radius. Haptic chime plays, unlocking Checkpoint 2. (In video, also demonstrate the desktop Demo simulation mode with visible badge).
- **Narration:**  
  > “The story doesn't advance with a tap. Deterministic geofencing unlocks the next chapter only when your boots actually reach the checkpoint.”

---

### Scene 6: Finale & Field Journal (0:60 – 0:75)
- **Visual:** Final checkpoint unlocked. Case Solved screen displays the mystery conclusion. User types a field note reflection and saves it to their device journal.
- **Narration:**  
  > “Traverse your neighborhood, solve the case, and record your field notes in a private on-device journal.”

---

### Scene 7: Outro (0:75 – 0:90)
- **Visual:** FIELDCRAFT logo, GitHub repository link, Hacktoberfest Week 1 badge.
- **Narration:**  
  > “FIELDCRAFT: Open-source, local AI, and real-world exploration. Step outside and touch grass.”
